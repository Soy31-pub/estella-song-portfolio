Case overviewLoewe · South Korea · 2022

Luxury attention was global. Korea’s path to purchase was not.

Loewe’s global content carried the brand world, but Korean consumers moved through a local ecosystem where Naver shaped search, content and shopping, while Kakao connected brands with high-value customers in daily conversation.

I developed a Korea channel strategy that gave each platform a specific job—then connected those jobs into one discovery-to-commerce journey.

Role
Digital Marketing Manager · Korea market and channel strategy
Scope

Team
Loewe, Assembly strategy and activation teams, Naver, Kakao and local partners

Market context

A different luxury operating system

In Korea, search, influence and shopping often happen inside the same local platform.

The opportunity was not to add more channels. It was to understand how Naver and Kakao changed the handoff between inspiration, product research, brand relationship and purchase.

The takeaway

“Localization is strongest when it changes the journey—not only the language.”

The global brand world stayed intact. What changed was how Korean consumers could find it, explore it, keep it close and move toward purchase.
