Case overviewAdobe · South Korea · 2021–2022

A mature product portfolio still needed a Korea-native growth system.

Adobe had global brand awareness and a broad product suite, but local growth depended on making each product useful in a specific Korean context. Translation alone could explain features; it could not always create relevance, trust or purchase intent.

I led Korea-market strategy and performance planning across local search, display, paid social, content and publisher ecosystems—turning consumer, creative and channel learning into connected quarterly decisions.

Role
Digital Marketing Manager · Korea connected strategy and client lead
Scope

Team
Adobe Korea and APAC, Assembly strategy, activation, search, analytics and operations teams, plus local media partners
01

Market context

Global familiarity was not local clarity

People knew Adobe. They did not always know which product fit their next task.

Customer and performance learning pointed to a gap between product recognition and practical understanding. Korea’s search-led discovery behavior made use-case clarity—and the handoff between local and global channels—critical.

Creative learning from FY22 Q1

Culturalized ideas beat translated assets when the use case was clear.

In a documented Korea ROI test, a culturalized creative direction delivered 55% higher return on media spend, 14% higher engaged rate and 81% lower cost per visit than the localized comparison. The learning was then carried into search copy, landing pages and future campaign briefs.

The useful contradiction

A social-native idea was not automatically a search-native idea.

TikTok was effective at young-audience discovery and engagement. But placing aespa imagery inside a high-intent Naver brand-search slot underperformed the previous offer-led creative. The lesson was not “celebrity works” or “celebrity fails”—it was to match the idea to the audience’s job on each platform.

How I ledKorea market · Strategy through learning

I turned fragmented channel results into one market growth narrative.

My contribution sat between the client’s business priorities and specialist delivery teams. I made sure search, social, display, content, landing-page and measurement decisions answered the same Korea-market question.

  • Converted consumer and performance signals into culturalized messaging and channel direction.
  • Connected global platform activity with Korea-specific publishers, search behavior and creative needs.
  • Used both winning and losing tests to clarify where each message belonged in the funnel.
  • Led client communication and quarterly synthesis so learning shaped the next plan.

The program was a cross-functional team effort. I led Korea connected strategy, client alignment and performance synthesis; specialist teams managed channel activation, analytics, search operations and asset delivery, while Adobe and its partners owned brand and artist assets.

The takeaway

“Culture earns attention when the product gives people something to do with it.”

The strongest growth system did not force one hero idea across every placement. It used culture to invite participation, product experience to build conviction, and high-intent channels to close the journey with clarity.
