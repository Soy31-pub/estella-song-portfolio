About me · The fuller picture

A social and cultural lens.
A commercial operator’s
mindset.

I grew from community and social storytelling into integrated growth, global brand strategy and Korea-market GTM—without losing curiosity about how people, platforms and culture shape one another.

Trained in journalism and strategic communication, I began my career building bilingual content, KOL partnerships and user growth for an international student platform in the US. At 1000heads, I learned to read social behavior across North America and Europe and turn it into platform-native campaigns for global technology and entertainment brands.

In Seoul, I expanded that foundation through integrated media, subscription growth, B2B acquisition, e-commerce and luxury localization for Adobe, LG, Loewe and Gucci. Most recently at iFLYTEK Korea, I moved upstream to own market entry: positioning, localization, channel strategy, launch operations and cross-functional decisions for AI products.

This range lets me move between the big picture and the operating detail—from finding a cultural tension or market opportunity to shaping the GTM plan, briefing creative and media, aligning stakeholders and learning from performance.

Outside the deck: digital nomad, part-time surfer and a lifelong observer of how culture travels.
