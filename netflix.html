Case overviewNetflix · Young-teen series launch

The audience existed inside the conversation. The challenge was learning how to hear it.

Netflix was preparing a new series for young teens—a generation beginning to form its own social language, creator relationships and fandom behavior.

As part of the 1000heads team, I supported audience research and launch planning that separated this group from the wider social conversation and turned its behavior into practical communication choices.

Role
Paid Media Executive · Social research and launch-planning support
Scope
Audience and competitor research, channel assessment, fandom signals and planning inputs
Team
Netflix and 1000heads strategy, data, social and media teams
01

Audience problem

Find the signal inside the noise

Demographics could name the audience. They could not explain how fandom moved.

The useful questions were behavioral: which shows, cast members and creators already mattered; what fans discussed; how they expressed belonging; and which channels made that behavior visible.

Translate social behavior into launch decisions.

My contributionResearch support · Planning input

I helped turn social evidence into questions the launch team could act on.

This was an earlier-career, collaborative project. My contribution supported the research and planning process rather than owning the full Netflix launch.

  • Supported audience, competitor and channel research around young-teen fandom.
  • Helped connect social signals with media, content and influencer planning questions.
  • Contributed to a social-first perspective that treated fans as participants—not passive reach.

Netflix and senior 1000heads strategy and data leads owned the overall brief and launch recommendations. I contributed research, channel and paid-media perspective within the wider team.

The takeaway

“Before choosing how to speak, learn how the audience already belongs.”

The strongest launch inputs came from the language, relationships and rituals young fans were already creating around entertainment.
