The same planning logic connected consumer attention and B2B demand while allowing audience, channel and next action to change by market.

01 · Prioritize

Choose where to grow

Compare category intent, audience scale and first-party behavior before assigning a market role.

02 · Sequence

Build the journey first

Match video, social, display and search depth to the audience’s next question and action.

03 · Carry forward

Connect media to sales

Use visits, lead forms, nurture and local follow-up to improve the next planning cycle.

Takeaway

Global consistency should live in the decision system—not identical execution.
