Case overviewLG Electronics · Global · 2021–2022

The product story was global. The reason to care had to be local.

LG’s portfolio spanned lightweight laptops, home appliances, medical displays and business projectors. Each launch needed a common strategic spine without flattening differences in market opportunity, audience intent or purchase journey.

I led connected strategy and client planning across consumer and B2B work—turning market, website and media signals into audience priorities, channel roles and a sequence from discovery to action.

Consumer launch spotlight

Go gram · May–June 2022 · Five markets

The film was global. The launch plan behaved market by market.

For the LG gram launch in the US, Canada, Germany, Spain and Taiwan, the strategy worked backward from a 100 million video-view objective while protecting the second job: turn attention into qualified visits to LG.com.

01

Tease

Short-form · Stories · In-stream

Open with lightweight, social-native motion to establish the “Go gram” spirit among tech and lifestyle audiences.

02

Explain

Mid-form · In-feed · Video sequence

Move from the launch promise to specific reasons to believe: portability, performance and battery life.

03

Re-engage

Long-form · Custom audience · Retargeting

Use video viewers, social engagers and product-page visitors to deepen the story and bring people back to LG.com.

What I owned

I made the global objective usable for five different market realities.

My role was to keep the audience logic, channel sequence and commercial destination connected—not to treat 100 million views as an isolated media target.

How I ledGlobal strategy · Local action

I connected product, market and channel specialists around one decision path.

My contribution sat between LG’s global ambition and the teams turning it into media, data and local-market action. I translated inputs into a story the client could use to decide where to invest, whom to prioritize and what to learn next.

  • Turned market, category and first-party signals into clear audience and channel choices.
  • Aligned consumer and B2B planning around the same journey logic without forcing the same tactics.
  • Connected activation results with nurture, sales feedback and the next planning cycle.
  • Extended the approach into a micro-segmentation roadmap using GMP, Ads Data Hub and iterative test-and-learn.

The work was delivered across LG, Assembly and local-market teams. I led connected strategy, client alignment and performance synthesis; specialists owned platform activation, analytics, data engineering and email operations, while LG sales teams owned lead qualification and opportunity follow-up.

The takeaway

“Global consistency should live in the decision system—not in identical market execution.”

A reusable GTM model did not mean repeating the same media plan. It meant asking the same hard questions in every market, then letting category intent, audience behavior and sales feedback change the answer.
